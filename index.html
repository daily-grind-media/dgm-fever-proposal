Understanding the brief.

Swiss Winter Village turns Liberty Station into an immersive European alpine holiday market — chalets, raclette and mulled wine, an ice rink, a vintage carousel, live performances and real guests moving through it all.

Fever needs a cinematographic, action-oriented capture — not conventional event coverage — producing striking, emotionally engaging photo and video assets ready for organic and paid marketing.
