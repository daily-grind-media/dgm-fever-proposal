02Our recommended direction

Foundation first. Then activation.

We’re deliberately building the assets, credibility and infrastructure that make future acquisition more effective.

Months 1 and 2 put the premium website, creative production, organic distribution, SEO and GEO, founder authority and conversion infrastructure in place. Advertising research, account audits, tracking evaluation and campaign preparation run at the same time. Month 3 is the planned activation of the approved paid strategy, subject to readiness and agreed investment.

Month 01Month 02Month 03
The foundationWebsite · production · organic · SEO/GEO · founder authority · conversion
BuildPremium site, creative, content, search and GHL infrastructure
Continue & optimizePublishing, SEO/GEO and conversion
AcquisitionResearch · audits · tracking · campaign preparation
PrepareAudit past advertising, evaluate tracking, build the strategy and campaigns
ActivatePlanned launch of the approved paid strategy

Why this order. Paid campaigns perform best when they point to a site, content and follow-up worth landing on. Building those first gives future spend more to work with.

This is a sequencing decision, not a claim that organic marketing delivers results faster than paid. Evaluating past campaigns doesn’t mean switching off anything that’s working.
