01 The Opportunity

Junkyard isn’t starting from zero.

In about two and a half years, this ownership group took over the venue, rebuilt it, and turned it into one of North County’s go-to spots for food, sports and nights out. The demand is real. Now there’s a second location on the horizon.

  • The VenueA full remodel that gives every guest a reason to stay longer.
  • The KitchenScratch-made wings, burgers, tacos and Junkyard Dogs, plus weekend brunch and 24 taps.
  • The Sports26 screens and every UFC event and major boxing card.
  • The NightsA seven-night calendar of live music, DJs, Latin night, trivia, karaoke and line dancing.
  • What’s NextAnother Junkyard location in planning.
The Coast News · Best of North County 2026. Gold for Best Sports Bar and Best Brunch, bronze for Best Hot Dog.
Where we come in

The marketing should keep pace with the business.

Right now a lot of the marketing runs through ownership. As the calendar and the footprint grow, that work grows with it.
